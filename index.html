⚙ EMI Interest Slabs & Rates
Progressive rates apply to the outstanding loan balance each period. Example: first ₹3,00,000 at 7%, next ₹2,00,000 at 8%, and any balance above ₹5,00,000 at 9%.
Slab EMI method: each installment calculates interest on the opening outstanding balance by slab. The EMI is solved so that the balance reaches zero at the end of the selected period. Example: ₹5,00,000 = ₹3,00,000 @ 7% + ₹2,00,000 @ 8%.
Both repayment types pay that period's interest on the outstanding balance first, then principal. With Equal installment, the total EMI is fixed and the interest/principal mix shifts each month. With Equal principal, the principal portion is fixed every month, so interest — and therefore the total installment — shrinks month by month. During a moratorium, no principal is repaid: choose whether you pay the interest in cash each month, or let it accrue and get added to the principal (capitalized) before EMI/installments begin.

LEDGER · 02 DEPOSITS

Deposit Calculators

Fixed / reinvestment deposits, recurring deposits, and SIPs — each with its own maturity ledger.

Reinvestment / cumulative schemes are FDs where interest is added back to principal instead of paid out. For non-cumulative FDs: quarterly payout pays the exact simple quarterly interest (P×R/4) with principal untouched, while monthly payout pays a discounted monthly rate — since you receive the money sooner, banks pay slightly less than a plain 1/12th of the annual interest, so that 12 monthly payouts work out equivalent to 4 quarterly ones. This mirrors standard practice at Indian banks (e.g. ICICI, SBI).
Compounding-period register
Interest is compounded quarterly on the running balance, in line with standard bank RD practice; the last part-quarter (if any) earns simple interest.

⚙ Interest Settings — Slabs & Rates
Each day's closing balance is progressively split across these slabs. Daily interest = daily product × annual rate ÷ 365. The slab amount, not the full balance, earns the slab rate.
Example: if the closing balance is ₹8,00,000, a ₹3,00,000 first slab is calculated at its rate, the next slab only on the next portion, and so on.
✓ Slab settings are saved automatically on this device and will not change when a statement is reloaded or recalculated. Use Reset built-in slabs to replace them.
